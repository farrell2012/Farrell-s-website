Carp fishing

This is the guide to carp fishing.

Carp fishing.Probably the most popular kind of fishing in the uk,They are big beautiful fish.
Carp in size can be from as small as a minnow,To over 60 pounds and are very nice looking fish,Although catching
a 60 pound carp in england is a once in a lifetime catch.And unlike in america where many carp species are
invasive in the UK they are very much respected.There are quite a couple different type of carp like:mirror
carp,common carp,f1,cruisian carp and ghost carp(made from a koi and common or mirror).Through my fishing i
have caught over 100 carp(most species,sadly not koi yet)and my pb was 9 pounds for over 2 years.Finally though
a couple of weeks ago i broke it by one pound,And That may not seem much but for so long i had been wanting to catch
a double figure carp and i successfully did.Another thing i have realised through my fishing is that most of the
time,Your biggest carp is usually going to be a mirror instead of a common.I am not sure if thats only for me but
i have seen alot of people`s pb`s being mirror carp.Another reason Carp are so popular in the UK is because they are
one of the most hard fighting fish in our waters(nearly as hard as catfish),And they practically eat every kind of bait
some popular examples to use are:

  • bread
  • corn
  • maggots
  • boilies
  • Pop-ups
  • wafters
  • hemp
  • tigernut

I have not put a picture for all baites as i would expect you already know what the rest look like.Although you may only know a
couple of these baits,As you prgress you will soon become very familiar with them.Carp are also a very common fish in the uk,And Unike
many other species you can probably find them in your local pond,Lake and maybe even river.My best advise is the confident approach.Go
down to your local tackle shop and ask what the best local spots are and if they have carp.This has worked for me in many occasions and
asking will 100 percent of the time help.The equipment i would recommend for starting carp fishing is a 10-12 foot carp rod,A size 5000
reel,15 pound monofilament line,a rod rest with a bite alarm and a good sized net.You can learn about all the small little bits on fish with
carls(fishing tutorial) youtube channel.Next is fish care equipment,This includes a 42" net (which nearly all lakes require you to have),
Also a landing mat or cradle(most of the time required),A weigh sling and scale,And a water bucket for the fish when out of water.I would also recommend
a chair and if you are doing overnight fishing,A bedchair and bivvi,Also potentially a stove for cooking food on.This type of fishing takes usually
alot of gear and money,But can still be good for beginners.I would recommend you start on coarse,And you will soon find out why.But before that its time
to mention probably the most controversial type of fishing.
