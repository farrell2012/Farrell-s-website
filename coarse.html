Coarse fishing

This is a guide to Coarse Fishing

Now,This is probably the best type of beginner fishing there is.Coarse fishing,Although many may see it as to easy.It is not and at
times can be as difficult as any other type of fishing if not harder,Since there are so many fish species that are under the coarse
fishing name,there are many different fish to target depending on what challenge level you want.Here is a list of the most common
species that i have caught that you can catch from easiest to most difficult:

  1. roach
  2. gudgeon
  3. rudd
  4. common carp
  5. mirror carp
  6. bream
  7. tench
  8. f1 carp
  9. chub
  10. catfish
  11. barbel
  12. grayling
  13. grass carp
  14. eel
  15. ide

although i have listed these fish from easiest to hardest to catch,As you go down the line they also match
with hardest to find.But some of these fish can be very rewarding and bring great excitement,The main three
i will be talking about are roach(which you usually use a float to catch),barbel(whcih you usually catch with
a feeder) and carp(which you usually use ledger to catch).But since i have already briefed over carp i will only
go in depth with roach and barbel fishing.

Roach/Float fishing

Float fishing,very fun and very easy to learn.All you have to do is tie on a float,split shot and hook
then you just put on a small bit of bait and you can start fishing.This is also the easiest way to catch
as with just this rig i have caught a fish in just over 10 seconds(which is insanely quick in fishing terms).
Although this rig seems simple and beginner,With this alone you can catch over half of the fish species out
of all the coarse fish.

barbel/feeder fishing

Feeder fishing,Although this type of fishing has more gear and skill than float fishing.It is still very beginner
friendly and many pro anglers use it too,For feeder fishing you will need (other than the essentials/rod,reel,line)
a method or cage feeder,A swivel,Something to keep the feeder in place like a splitshot or bead,Hooklink material,Small
hook and some bait like a pellet,wafter,pop-up,corn and method feeder mix(any flavour)




There are also two different kinds of ways you can coarse fish,
Either with a rod(which is used in every type of fishing) or you can use a pole:

Pole fishing

Pole fishing is usually the more expensive option,But can open up a whole new view of fishing for you.I only had ever
used a rod for all of my fishing,Until my grandpa took me to try out the pole and i loved it.At first I thought
it would be so different,And to no surprise ,It was in all ways good.I genuinely was starting to prefer it to a normal carp
setup,But after a while I realised that it isnt always that fun.Sometimes with fast-flowing water and minor issues,like weather,
It can really affect the fishing.I would recommend you look into it though before quickly brushing it off your shoulder
