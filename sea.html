Sea fishing

This is a guide to Sea Fishing

Sea fishing,This is the last type of fishing to be mentioned in this website.I`ve only sea fished once
and it was quite fun,I did it from shore.Although you can use a boat or pier to get further/To deeper water,
where there are predominantly bigger fish the more far out you are or cast.One small downside that you might have
when sea fishing is,If you are fishing on shore you will usually have to come at high tide which is not that
long of a time period.And if you dont come near,The sea will be far out and practically impossible to cast
to.There are to many different fish species in the sea tolist them all so i will just name the ones you will most
likely catch,Which are:

  • Mackerel
  • Bass
  • Cod
  • Pollack
  • Whiting
  • Flounder
  • Wrasse

These are all good beginner fish and have some different fishing techniques for each,Just like lure fishing
the different types of sea fishing are using lures,dead bait or live bait.There is no best method out of the three
,As each technique is good for different fish types/species.Lets go a bit more in depth on what each of these
fishing styles are:

Lure sea fishing

"Lure sea fishing is using an imitational bait to catch preditory fish from the sea
,Like bass and mackerel.This could be hard baits,jerk baits,soft plastic jig heads,There
are many different kinds of lures you can use in the sea.So I would recommend you buy a
small amount of different types and test which workes best on the specific water you are
fishing in.

Dead bait sea fishing

Dead bait sea fishing is also another very affective way to fish.With this you can practically
use any saltwater species like crab,shrimp or even fish that you catch and then send deep on a hook.
Like mackerel or pollack and go for bigger fish and maybe even sharks.Just test all different kinds of
bait depending on your budget.

Live bait sea fishing

Live bait sea fishing is probably the easiest type of sea fishing,As the bait practically do
all the work for you.All you have to do is get your live bait either from the sea or a shop put it on a
hook or two and send it out far with some line to swim/go around in the water.you can literally use any
bait for this kind as long as it is obviously alive.
