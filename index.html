Some info about me.

I first got into fishing at the ripe age of 10 and started on carp fishing.
Although i felt it was a good starting point for me,I had not yet realised that
there were much easier ways of fishing.So my advise to you is start on float fishing
,Which is mostly for coarse fishing species.It is probably the easiest type of fishing there
is,And is a great stepping stone to decide whether you want to invest more time into
the sport(And yes.fishing is a sport).The way i found out about float fishing is through a
youtube channel,Fish with carls channel and in his channel he has tutorials and nearly every
kind of fishing.But i would recommend you watch his Float fishing for beginners video.
There are quite a bit of species you can catch on a float like:

  • roach
  • carp
  • chub
  • rudd
  • dase
  • bream
  • tench

As you can probably tell there are many species you can catch on such simple baits,
But one main thing to remember is that patience is key and sometimes the fish may take
long to find your bait.I wont spoil all the info though since most of it is in the coarse
fishing section so what are you waiting for
GO CHECK IT OUT
