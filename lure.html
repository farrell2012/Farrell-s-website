Lure fishing

This is the guide to lure fishing

Lure fishing,you`ll either love it or hate it.This type of fishing can be very tricky but if you find the right spot you can unlock so
much potential.I would definitely not recommend this type of fishing for beginners,Although it does not cost as much as carp fishing
,Many people argue that it takes alot more skill.Lure fishing is all about moving around and casting through as much water as possible,
Giving you the best chance of hooking a fish.For lure fishing you use imitational fish that are plastic,To attract and decieve preditor fish.
Species that you can catch on a lure are:

  • pike
  • perch
  • chub

Pike:

pike are the biggest freshwater predator in the uk and can grow up to 40 pounds in size.Usually in england the average size is 1-20 pounds,
In my fishing life i have not yet caught a pike but i know many people who have and i know alot about them.There are two different ways you
can catch this species, 1.lures or 2.dead bait.Its probably self explainitory on what these baits are.With dead bait you can use small fish
like minnows,baby carp and the most common roach.These you can either catch yourself or buy them frozen,Its your choice and preferance on
what bait you choose,but its best to find the most affective for you.With dead bait you can either use a dead bait float or fish it on the bottom.
Both ways are as affective as the other.

Perch:

Perch are probably the best fish to try start carching when lure fishing,As they are smaller and more cost effective than pike.
They average on around a pound but can grow upto just below 6lb,Catching a 6 pounder in England though would be a 1 in a billion
chance as they omly really grow to 4lb in the UK.For perch I would advise you either use a soft plastic jig head

or small spinner.

Chub:

Chub,To be honest I have never specifically fished for chub specifically.
But i still know alot about them,They can grow upto a wopping 9 pounds,
But average at around 1-5 pounds. This fish species eat other fish and natural
